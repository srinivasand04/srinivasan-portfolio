Dynamic Tactical AI — Football Tracking Data
Problem: Can a model read tactics from real match tracking data — where players will move, who will receive the ball, and which pass gives the best chance?
Approach
Stack
Outcome
  • Put 7 real DFL / Sportec matches and 36 simulated Google Research Football games into one common format (105 × 68 m, 10 frames/s)
